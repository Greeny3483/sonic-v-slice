## MEDALS GUIDE (PC EXCLUSIVE)
Welcome to a small, but expanding list of medals and instructions on how to get them.
Please note that the medals are purely cosmetic, and (right now) there is no benefit from getting them.

*Notice for prior mod users, if you've already completed the task before this update, the achievements cannot be given to you without a save data reset.*

*In instances like **Beyond the Black Sun** and **The Curse**, the medals are only granted after the second song if: **(1)** the second song is NOT completed, and **(2)** the prior song IS completed.*

- **Faster Than Fear**
  - Awarded for completing the **VS SONIC.EXE** week.
- ***Secret Medal***
  - ??
- **Beyond the Black Sun**
  - Awarded for completing **Faker** and **Black Sun**
- **The Curse**
  - Awarded for completing **Sunshine** and **Soulless**
- **I Die Hard**
  - Awarded for completing **Prey** and **Fight or Flight**
- **Silent Night**
  - Awarded for completing **Slaybells**
- **I Fixed You : )**
  - Awarded for completing **Relax**, **Round-A-Bout**, and **Spike Trap**
